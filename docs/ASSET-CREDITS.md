## Notes

- **Kenney packs:** CC0 (public domain). Credit is not required but is appreciated.
- **LPC (Liberated Pixel Cup) sprites:** licences vary per layer (mostly CC-BY-SA 3.0/4.0, OGA-BY or GPL). Credit each author, link the source and keep derived art under the same licence. The generator can export a credits file; save it here.
